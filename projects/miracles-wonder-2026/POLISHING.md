# Polishing / Anti-Slop Recommendations
Date: 2026-09-30
Status: recommendation, not gate

## Rise above sea of slop
Slop = generic, frictionless, hidden process. We = specific, friction, shown work.

1. Human anchor without accordion. You do not play accordion, fine. Use: hired impressionists for Price/Lorre homages (credited, breath kept), one live take of anything you do play, or real field recordings (FPV whirr, room tone, Vienna dawn). One real element beats all-synth.

2. Simulated friction (easy). Uneven timing +/-12ms on hats and bass, slight detune +/-8 cents on pads, second ALWAYS sharp +10 cents screamed, rolling rolling second hit late 30ms. Imperfection reads human.

3. Detune to atonal at ball. YES. Arc: 92 BPM tonal folk -> 138 tense chromatic -> ball = hysterical staccato, strings detune to clusters, voices to atonal scream on rolling rolling, no beat. Collapse tonality as ball rolls. Then CODA resolves: kalimba back to major, butterflies, Price whisper tonal. Atonal dread makes mercy land.

4. Specificity moat. Keep codons, Mahia, Bostrom, Kharkiv, Livermore. Slop says DNA city danger. We name.

5. Stillness over morph. Black ball: 1 held Kitoh-study panel 6s, not 500 variations. Drone: 1 locked thermal hold, not morph sludge.

6. Show work. Publish footnotes as liner. Process transparency is anti-slop.
